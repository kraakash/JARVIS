**Definition:** Ek general ek tarah ki strategy ya plan hai jo kisi bhi kshetra mein upyogi ho sakta hai. Yah ek general ko apne karyon ko pura karne ke liye ek anumaanit plan banaye rakhne ka kaam karta hai.

**Real Example:** Khaas kar sports me, ek general coach ko ek team ko jeetne ke liye ek strategy banane ki jarurat hoti hai. Usse team ke khiladiyon ke sthan aur unke kushalataon ko dhyan mein rakhkar ek plan banaana hota hai.

**How it Works:** Ek general ke liye pratyek kadam ke liye ek strategy tayaar karna hota hai. Yahaan kuch charan hain:

1. Prashn utpann karna: General ko kya karna hai aur uska uddeshya kaanunon aur lakshyon ko samajhna hota hai.
2. Data kharidna aur analysis karna: General ko apne data aur knowledge ke aadhaar par ek strategy tayaar karna hota hai.
3. Plan banaana: General ek anumaanit plan banaata hai, jismein pratyek kadam ke liye vyavastha hoti hai.
4. Karyon ko pura karna: General apne team ya aapne aap ko apne plan ke mutabik kaam karta hai.

**Applications:** General ka upayog kai kshetron mein hota hai, jaise:

* Military me: General apne sainikon ko jeetne ke liye ek strategy banate hain.
* Sports me: Coach ek team ko jeetne ke liye ek strategy banate hain.
* Business me: General manager apne company ki strategy banate hain.
* Jeevan me: Har vyakti apne jeevan mein ek general ka role play karta hai, jaise ki apne parivaar aur doston ke liye ek strategy banana.
